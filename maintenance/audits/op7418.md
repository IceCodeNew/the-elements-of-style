## Conflicts found

1. **Dashes.** blader v3.0.0 §8 bans em and en dashes in the final text unless the writer's sample uses them. stop-slop says "No em dashes" and "Em-dash anywhere? Remove it." op7418 OP-C08 keeps dashes that explain, insert or turn, even without a sample. **Resolution:** counting or banning dashes is X-DEAI. The conditional "a dash that hides the clause relation → state the relation" is BOTH. The Chinese dash form (——, two em widths) is a ZH punctuation matter.
2. **Quotation marks.** blader §21 straightens curly quotes (“…” → "…"). op7418 OP-C21 does the opposite in Chinese prose ("…" → “…”). **Resolution:** language-specific. In ZH, full-width “” (or 「」 when specified) is the norm, so the rule goes to ZH. For EN, quote style follows the house style, and blader's version is X-DEAI.
3. **Passive voice and subjects.** stop-slop rule 3 says "Every sentence needs a human subject… No passive constructions." blader §11's After example invents "The system". op7418 OP-C11 and OP-C28 keep the passive when the agent is unknown or unimportant, or the genre needs it, and forbid inventing a subject. **Resolution:** BOTH, in op7418's conditional form. The absolute version also clashes with JA and ZH, where dropping the subject is grammatical, and with TECH, where a system as the actor is normal.
4. **Sentence-length rhythm.** blader v3.0.0 step 4 ("Vary sentence length") and stop-slop rule 6 and its quick check ("Three consecutive sentences match length? Break one") clash with op7418 OP-V06 ("do not split or merge just to vary length"). **Resolution:** the frequency rules are X-DEAI, and op7418's guard is BOTH.
5. **Adding voice.** blader v3.0.0 Voice allows adding "a reaction where the writer would". op7418 OP-V01 and OP-P01 forbid adding experiences, emotions or conclusions. **Resolution:** PERSONAL takes op7418's form. Adding a reaction is acceptable only when the user asks for new content, and it is then labeled as new (OP-P02).
6. **Specificity.** stop-slop rule 4 ("Be specific. Name the specific thing.") against op7418 OP-P05 ("stay general when the source has no detail; never invent"). **Resolution:** keep both as one BOTH rule: "Be specific with what you have; with nothing, stay general or ask."
7. **Connectives.** blader §12 lists "additionally" as an AI word, and earlier lists include moreover and furthermore. op7418 OP-V04 keeps connectives that mark order, simultaneity or contrast. **Resolution:** the conditional OP-V04 is BOTH; banning a connective as a vocabulary tell is X-DEAI.
8. **Qualifiers and adverbs.** stop-slop says "Kill [all] adverbs", while op7418 OP-C09 keeps qualifiers that carry evidence strength, scope or legal meaning. **Resolution:** BOTH, conditional (compress only repeated hedges on the same uncertainty).
9. **Triads.** stop-slop says "Two items beat three", while op7418 OP-C06 prescribes no count. **Resolution:** the count rule is X-DEAI, and the content rule is BOTH.
10. **Bold.** blader §19 says "Remove the bold" with no condition, while op7418 OP-C19 keeps bold needed for skimming and warnings. **Resolution:** BOTH, conditional.
11. **Heading case.** blader §20 says "Use sentence case", while op7418 OP-C20b follows the target format. **Resolution:** EN, conditional on the house style.
12. **Heading restated in the first sentence.** blader §24 deletes the restating sentence outright. op7418's own After example for OP-C24 keeps one "本节介绍导出限制。" and deletes only the duplicate. **Resolution:** BOTH with the "no independent meaning" condition. A single scope sentence is conventional in JA (本章では…) and ZH technical writing, and the target should say so.
13. **Output and scoring.** blader returns a draft, the remaining patterns and the final. stop-slop scores 1–10 on 5 dimensions and revises below 35. op7418 OP-O01 and OP-K08 deliver the final text only and use no self-score. **Resolution:** all of these are X-SCOPE.
14. **Dating text.** blader's "When not to act" says text written before 2022-11-30 is not AI-written, while op7418 OP-U01 says not to judge by date. **Resolution:** both are X-SCOPE (authorship detection).
15. **Internal: list items and the structure check.** SKILL.md L51 protects only "列表项的独立含义和顺序" (each list item's meaning and order), so the item text may be reworded. `tests/check_structure.py` requires numbered-step lines and table rows to be byte-identical. **Resolution:** the writing rule follows SKILL.md (OP-O05 → OP-P01).
